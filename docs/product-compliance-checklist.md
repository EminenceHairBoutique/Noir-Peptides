# Noir Peptides Product Compliance Checklist

Before any product is published, confirm the following:

## Product Identity

- Product name is accurate
- Vial size is listed
- Form is listed
- Storage condition is listed
- Molecular weight is listed where available
- Peptide sequence is listed where available
- CAS number is listed where applicable
- Batch number is listed where available

## Documentation

- COA is available or marked as pending
- COA matches the product and batch
- Supplier documentation is stored internally
- Safety or handling information is stored internally
- Product image does not imply medical, human, or injection use

## Copy Review

Product page does not include:

- Human-use claims
- Veterinary-use claims
- Dosing
- Injection
- Ingestion
- Treatment language
- Disease language
- Fitness claims
- Fat loss claims
- Libido claims
- Anti-aging body claims
- Injury recovery claims
- Healing claims
- Before/after claims
- Testimonials about bodily effects

## Category Review

Product is placed only in a safe research-domain category:

- Tissue Research
- GH-Axis Research
- Neurochemical Research
- Metabolic Pathway Research
- Cellular Aging Research
- Receptor Signaling Research
- Reference Materials

## Required Site Placement

Confirm product page includes:

- Research-use disclaimer
- COA badge if available
- Batch number if available
- Storage condition
- Purity information if available
- “What This Product Is Not” section
- No dosage, cycle, or human-use guidance

## Checkout

Confirm:

- Cart displays research-use disclaimer
- Checkout requires qualified purchaser acknowledgment
- Order metadata records acknowledgment where supported
- Order confirmation repeats research-use disclaimer

## Final Approval

No product should go live until it passes copy, documentation, checkout, and legal/compliance review.
